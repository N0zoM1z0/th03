## Lifecycle

The accepted score and Yumemi bomb descriptors are validated first. Each fresh
round compiles the accepted bomb under its original default carrier, then warning,
effects and both background translation units under their maintained paths.
Only afterward does the exact reviewed root transform and existing -j1 carrier
transition occur. The existing Schedule and normal Tup generated dependencies,
root assembly and link are unchanged. No cached tail object or include wrapper
substitutes for a fresh maintained translation unit.

The expected 1,553 relocation stream is frozen before native execution, SHA256
ca8c5677d1b4cb31637142a280bb491e6823ca23c3372a168824b9dd2dfcab98.
Actual fresh root and producer OMF structure is verified after normal Tup build,
independently of linked bytes and before linked success contributes to acceptance.
This is not a prelink actual-root proof. Structural failure remains fatal even
if the retained executable bytes match.
