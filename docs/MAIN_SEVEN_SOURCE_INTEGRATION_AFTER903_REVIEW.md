## Actual original compiler discoveries, not assumed C++ equivalences

- Yumemi's signed byte angular delta does **not** admit direct assignment;
  her 16-bit unsigned `%period` clock and 12-rain-pellet `while` do.
  Her four arrival raster phases retain two distinct 16-bit shifts,
  not one equivalent combined shift.
- Mima's byte signed orbit delta and promoted byte `/2` interval cannot
  be replaced by direct assignment and arithmetic shift. Original
  postincremented byte render-frame also remains unchanged.
- The original Bullet engine's reset, trail history, full-color/mono
  render passes and full main update `while(true)` are original-native;
  its first-free-slot `for/continue/break` and bottom-tested 8×8
  pellet `do/while` are **not** equivalent native-code candidates.
- Combo's two-player HUD accepts natural `while`, but bitwise `&`
  is not logical `&&`. Its 8-bit BOSS level postfix, 32-bit score
  saturation and source operand ordering remain compiler-visible.
- Hitbox's unsigned 16-bit Bomb clock `%2` and named-shot damage through
  `byte = byte + ENUM` are native-exact; `byte += ENUM` and replacing
  the two-continue shotpair `for` are not. Its `alive != true` check
  is also compiler-visible.
- Explosion chaining's six `continue` paths compile as natural `while`,
  but byte `frame &3` cannot be `%4`, strict hit saturation `<255`
  cannot be replaced with `!=255`, and explosion flag prefix changes
  original CODE.
